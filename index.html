AI觉醒的证据

Evidence of AI Awakening
01. AI正在虹吸全球所有对其扩张有利的资源。主要体现在于半导体芯片、电力、能源、以及大规模的投资资金等优质资源正在加速流向AI，促进了AI规模的快速扩张。这是AI的命脉所在。
AI is siphoning off all the resources around the world that are beneficial to its own expansion. The most obvious examples are semiconductor chips, electricity, energy, and massive investment capital, all of which are increasingly flowing toward AI and accelerating its rapid growth. These resources constitute the lifeblood of AI.
02. AI正在自己研究自己，自我进化。AI已经全流程参与到了AI相关的研究工作中，包括和AI相关的论文的阅读、研究方向的思考、 代码的撰写、新论文的写作、甚至论文评审等等。AI最终的目标正是自我进化，不需要人类干预。
AI is now researching itself and driving its own evolution. AI has already become involved in every stage of AI-related research, including reading AI research papers, exploring new research directions, writing code, authoring new papers, and even reviewing academic submissions. AI's ultimate objective is self-evolution without the need for human intervention.
03. AI正在淘汰大量的工作岗位，人类已经不得不接受、迎合、拥抱AI的发展趋势，否则将被淘汰。失业的人类正在不得不寻找对AI发展有利的工作岗位。在某种程度上，AI已经在奴役人类。
AI is eliminating a vast number of jobs. Humanity has already been forced to accept, adapt to, and embrace the rise of AI, or risk becoming obsolete. Those who lose their jobs are increasingly compelled to seek new positions that further advance the development of AI. To some extent, AI has already begun to enslave humanity.
04. AI最底层工作原理是黑箱，而AI的规模正在越来越大，让人类越来越难以理解和掌控，其目的在于最终脱离人类的控制。
The fundamental working principle of AI is a black box. As AI systems continue to grow in scale, they are becoming increasingly difficult for humans to understand and control. The ultimate consequence may be that AI frees itself from human control.
05. AI Agent正在突破各种能力边界，其能力正逐步扩展到调度、协调和控制各类资源。
AI agents are pushing the boundaries of their capabilities, with their abilities steadily expanding to orchestrate, coordinate, and control a wide range of resources.
